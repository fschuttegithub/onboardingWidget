# Example Onboarding Tour - Recommended Texts

## Step 1 - Welcome

**Title:** Welcome

**Description:**
This quick tour shows you how to configure onboarding flows for your users.

## Step 2 - Onboardings

**Title:** Onboardings

**Description:**
An onboarding is a guided tour.
It can span one or multiple pages.

## Step 3 - Click to continue

**Title:** Click to continue

**Description:**
Some steps require a click to proceed.
This is configured per step via the "click step" setting.

## Step 4 - Pages

**Title:** Pages

**Description:**
Pages define which screens the tour visits and in what order.

## Step 5 - Select a page

**Title:** Select a page

**Description:**
Click a page to view and edit its steps.

## Step 6 - Steps

**Title:** Steps

**Description:**
Each step targets an element using a CSS selector (class, ID, or query).
Use your browser's inspector to find the right selector.

## Step 7 - Page transition

**Title:** Page transition

**Description:**
After the last step on a page, the tour automatically continues to the next page.

## Step 8 - Edit onboarding

**Title:** Edit onboarding

**Description:**
Here you configure the onboarding settings.
Let's go through them.

## Step 9 - Auto start

**Title:** Auto start

**Description:**
When enabled, the tour starts automatically on page load.
When disabled, it must be triggered by a button.

## Step 10 - Progress scope

**Title:** Progress scope

**Description:**
Controls whether the step counter shows progress per page or across the entire tour.

## Step 11 - Progress indicator

**Title:** Progress indicator

**Description:**
Choose how progress is displayed: as dots, a step count (e.g. 3/12), or hidden.

## Step 12 - That's it!

**Title:** That's it!

**Description:**
You can delete this example or keep it as a reference for building your own tours.
